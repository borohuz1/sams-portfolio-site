license text is in [license.md](license.md).

## What is Bruno Simon's work

Everything that makes this a world rather than a page:

- The 3D island, the track layout, the terrain and the scenery
- The drivable car and the vehicle physics
- The engine: the render loop, the physics, the areas, the camera
- The career stone slabs, the workshop screens, the lab shelf, the statue area
- The UI, the menu, the map, the achievements system
- All the models, textures and sounds in `static/`

## What is mine

- Every word: the name, the role, the bio, the six zones, the tool list, the
  project descriptions, the lab experiments, the contact details
- The zone label plates in `static/career/`
- The project screens in `static/projects/images/`
- The lab shelf images in `static/lab/images/`
- The removal of the original author's props from the world model, so the island
  does not show somebody else's name or accounts
